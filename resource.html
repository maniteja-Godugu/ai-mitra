Free Learning Resources

Learning about Artificial Intelligence becomes easier when you have the right resources. AI Mitra brings together simple, student-friendly learning materials designed to help you understand AI concepts, discover useful AI tools and communicate effectively with AI. Whether you are just beginning your AI journey or exploring new possibilities, these resources will help you learn step by step. Explore, practise and grow your understanding of AI with AI Mitra.
