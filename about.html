About AI Mitra

Making Artificial Intelligence easier to understand, explore and use responsibly.

AI Mitra is a student-led educational initiative created to make Artificial Intelligence easier to understand and accessible to young learners. Our goal is to introduce students to AI through simple explanations, relatable examples, practical activities and awareness programmes. We believe that understanding AI should not be limited to technical experts. Every student deserves an opportunity to explore how AI works, where it is used and how to use it responsibly. Through learning resources, workshops and community involvement, AI Mitra encourages curiosity, creativity and critical thinking, helping students become informed and confident participants in an AI-powered world.
